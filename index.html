What this prototype models

RoR-derived normal-beam forces, plastic deformation and breaking. New experimental contact handling between structural nodes and triangle surfaces. Synthetic, uncalibrated vehicle cages with a reinforced cabin.

Gravity and tire dynamics are disabled in the demos to isolate impact behavior. Wheels are visual markers. This is not the complete RoR engine or a validated reconstruction tool.
